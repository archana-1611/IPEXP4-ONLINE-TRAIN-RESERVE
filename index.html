Railway Ticket Refund & Cancellation Rules

Official cancellation charges based on date of reservation, class type, and hours prior to departure.

Tier 1 • > 48 Hours Before Departure

Flat Cancellation Deduction

Per Passenger Flat Fee:
• 1A / Executive: ₹240 + GST
• 2A: ₹200 + GST
• 3A / CC: ₹180 + GST
• Sleeper (SL): ₹120
Remaining fare is 100% refunded to passenger account.

Tier 2 • 48 to 12 Hours Before Departure

25% Deduction

25% of total ticket fare is deducted subject to minimum flat cancellation charge per passenger. 75% is refunded.

Tier 3 • 12 to 4 Hours Before Departure

50% Deduction

50% of the total ticket fare is deducted up to chart preparation time. 50% is refunded.

Tier 4 • Less than 4 Hours / After Charting

0% Refund (No Refund)

No refund is granted on confirmed tickets cancelled after chart preparation or within 4 hours of scheduled departure.

RAC & Waitlisted Tickets

Clerkage Charge Only

Only clerkage fee of ₹60 per passenger is deducted if cancelled up to 30 minutes before scheduled departure. Remainder is credited instantly.
